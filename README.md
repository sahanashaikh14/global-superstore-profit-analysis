# global-superstore-profit-analysis
Retail managers frequently use discounts to increase sales, but poorly planned discounting can reduce overall profitability. This project analyzes historical retail transaction data to identify profit drivers and to support data-driven pricing and discount decisions.

# Dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fatihilhan/global-superstore-dataset

Dataset: Global Superstore Dataset (Kaggle) he dataset contains historical retail transaction data including sales, profit, discount, quantity, product category, and regional information.

# Research Questions
RQ-1 Which product categories, sub-categories, discounts, and quantities are most strongly associated with higher profit?
RQ-2 How accurately can future profit be predicted from historical sales data such as discount, quantity, category, and region?
RQ-3 Can sales transactions be classified as profitable or non-profitable in advance to support discount and pricing decisions?
